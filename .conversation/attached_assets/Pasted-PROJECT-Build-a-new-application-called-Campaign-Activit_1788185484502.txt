WEBINAR SCHEDULE

When the user selects the webinar date, automatically create:

Recruitment branch:
- Invitation 1 at D-21, 10:00 a.m. audience local time
- Invitation 2 at D-14, 10:00 a.m.
- Invitation 3 at D-7, 10:00 a.m.
- Final invitation at D-1, 10:00 a.m.

Registrant branch:
- Immediate registration confirmation
- Reminder at 24 hours before the webinar
- Reminder at 30 minutes before the webinar
- Optional starting-now reminder

Post-event branches:
- Thank-you communication for attendees on the next business day
- Sorry-we-missed-you communication for registered no-shows on the next business day
- Optional recording reminder at D+7
- Optional handraiser CTA for attendees

Registration must immediately suppress remaining recruitment communications.

Attendance must determine whether a person enters the attendee or no-show branch.

A handraiser may only be created when someone explicitly submits the handraiser request. Attendance by itself is an engagement signal, not an explicit handraiser.

BUSINESS-DAY RULES

For pre-event communications:

- if the calculated date is Saturday, Sunday or an applicable holiday, move to the previous valid business day
- never move a recruitment communication later because of a weekend
- record the original calculated date and adjustment reason

For post-event communications:

- if the calculated date is Saturday, Sunday or an applicable holiday, move to the next valid business day
- never move a post-event communication to before the webinar

Support configurable regional holiday calendars.

After schedule adjustments, validate:

- duplicate communications
- portfolio frequency caps
- minimum spacing
- communication collisions
- event scheduled on a weekend or holiday
- insufficient recruitment lead time

Do not silently resolve collisions.

EVENT RESCHEDULING

The webinar date is the schedule anchor.

When it changes:

1. Calculate proposed new dates and times
2. Show an impact preview with old and new schedules
3. Require confirmation
4. Move draft and unsent activities
5. Do not move sent activities
6. Retain manually pinned activities and show a warning
7. Recalculate weekend, holiday and frequency rules
8. Update date and time tokens
9. Update scheduled platform jobs when integrations exist
10. Record the complete change in audit history

If the event has already been promoted, preserve original URLs, codes and attribution mappings. Do not silently replace distributed UTM identities.

INTERNAL TITLES

Generate internal titles from governance-controlled rules.

Recommended human-readable webinar pattern:

[Product or Topic] | [YYYY-MM-DD] | Webinar | [Subject] | [Target Profile]

Example:

Private Assets | 2026-07-26 | Webinar | Private Credit Outlook | Asset Owner CIOs

Generate communication titles by appending purpose and relative timing:

Private Assets | 2026-07-26 | Webinar | Private Credit Outlook | Invitation 01 D-21

Maintain a shorter calendar title separately.

Do not use ambiguous dates such as “July 26” when the meaning could be the day or year.
